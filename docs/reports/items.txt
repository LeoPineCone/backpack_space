# Items

Question: How much stronger does a build get from each item, compared with what the item costs? Which items are too strong or too weak, and which do the bots never use?
Accuracy: normal, seed 1. builds 50 (builds that receive the item), lobbies 30 (lobbies that supply builds and opponents), fights 8 (fights per build, with and without the item).
Bots: use the measured item ratings in data/sim/ratings.json (384 items).
Game data: data/*.json as it is now. More accuracy: --accuracy precise or --sims N; another sample: --seed S.

gain: points of win share a build gains when it gets the item for free, tested on builds of the round where the item is typically new (±: one standard error).
value: gain compared with what shop items of that round give per gold (1.0 = worth its price; crafted items are priced at the sum of their inputs). STRONG/WEAK: outside 0.5–1.5 even after noise; with '?': may be noise.
owned: share of bot runs that ever held the item (hero items: runs of that hero).
