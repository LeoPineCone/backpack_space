Reading: "wins" = share of lobby fights won against other heroes. "run wins" and "survives run" follow the real rules (4 lives).
"path items" = share of the final build's item cost that belongs to the path. "healed+shielded" is per fight, in percent of the hero's health.
A path carries when it wins at least 42 % and is within 6 points of the hero's best path.
