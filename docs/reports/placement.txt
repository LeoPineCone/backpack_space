# Placement

Question: How much does a build lose when the same items lie in the same bags at random instead of where the stars and bags help them?
Accuracy: normal, seed 1. builds 150 (builds per round), lobbies 20 (lobbies that supply builds and opponents), fights 10 (fights per build and layout).
Bots: use the measured item ratings in data/sim/ratings.json (384 items).
Game data: data/*.json as it is now. More accuracy: --accuracy precise or --sims N; another sample: --seed S.
