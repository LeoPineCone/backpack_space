# Recipes

Question: Which recipes do the bots complete, in which round, and how deep do the chains get in a run?
Accuracy: normal, seed 1. lobbies 50 (lobbies of 16 bots playing all 12 rounds).
Bots: use the measured item ratings in data/sim/ratings.json (384 items).
Game data: data/*.json as it is now. More accuracy: --accuracy precise or --sims N; another sample: --seed S.

Rules: at most 3 steps in a chain, at most 3 inputs. 151 recipes, 8 of them with 3 inputs.
Combinations per run: 10.2. Rounds in which an owned recipe did not lie together before the fight: 1.9 per run. Deepest chain step reached in a run: none 0 %, step 1 57 %, step 2 43 %, step 3 0 %.

Bots merge by the player's rules: they lay owned inputs next to each other and the recipe merges after that round's fight. They value an item higher when it completes a recipe but do not plan chains, so read the shares as a lower bound.
