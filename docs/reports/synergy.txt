# Hero synergies and matchups

Question: Do the heroes' rules and items interlock more with every round, and does one hero beat all others or do they counter each other?
Accuracy: normal, seed 1. lobbies 40 (lobbies of 16 bots (4 per hero, one per path plus one open bot) playing all 12 rounds), duels 300 (fights per hero pair and stage), pathDuels 120 (fights per path pair), builds 40 (builds per hero and round for the interlock test), opponents 12 (opponents per build in that test).
Bots: use the measured item ratings in data/sim/ratings.json (384 items).
Game data: data/*.json as it is now. More accuracy: --accuracy precise or --sims N; another sample: --seed S.

## 3. Do the builds interlock step by step?

"linked" = share of the build's items that feed another item or are fed by one: a passive on its stars or on the whole backpack,
a star trigger, a count of other items, or a stack (Burn, Toxin, Power on weapons, ...) that another item of the build produces.
"hero rules" = share of items the hero's own rules act on (Toxin on meals, speed for organic, mechanical or crafted items, pets, Power per phase).
"kit worth" and "synergy worth": 40 builds per cell fight 12 opponents of the same round three times with the same seeds:
as they are, without the hero's rules (no passives, no Supernova resistance), and without item-to-item effects. The drop in win share is the worth.
